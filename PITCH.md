# 💡 Pitch Deck — AI SALESMAN
*Empowering India's FMCG Distribution with Voice-First AI*

---

## 🎯 The Problem
- **12 Million+ Kirana Stores** form the backbone of Indian retail ($500B+ FMCG market).
- **Communication Chaos:** 80% of B2B replenishment orders happen over chaotic WhatsApp voice notes, phone calls, and scribbled paper notes.
- **Dialect & Unit Nuance:** Shopkeepers order in Hindi, Marathi, or Gujarati using regional terms: "3 peti", "2 khoka", "1 darjan".
- **Lost Revenue:** Sales reps spend 4+ hours daily manually typing orders into ERPs, leading to 12% stock misallocations and delayed dispatches.

---

## 🚀 The Solution: AI Salesman
An AI-powered voice ordering and distributor management platform:
1. **Understands Natural Indian Speech:** Transcribes code-mixed Hinglish and Marathi using **Sarvam AI (Saaras v3)**.
2. **SKU-Aware Conversions:** Automatically maps colloquial quantities to SKU packaging rules (never assumes uniform carton sizes).
3. **Automated Verification:** Validates warehouse stock, calculates distributor pricing, and applies trade schemes in milliseconds.
4. **Voice Back Confirmation:** Speaks order totals back to the shopkeeper using **Sarvam Bulbul v3 TTS**.
5. **Distributor Command Center:** Provides automated dispatch tracking and an AI Business Assistant for operational intelligence.

---

## 🏆 Innovation & Unfair Advantage
- **Voice-First Native UX:** Zero typing required for non-tech-savvy retailers.
- **High-Fidelity Indian LLM Stack:** Built on Sarvam AI models tailored specifically for Indian languages.
- **Fail-Safe Integrity:** AI generated drafts require explicit retailer confirmation before touching ERP inventory.

---

## 📈 Market Size & Business Model
- **TAM:** $50B Indian B2B FMCG distribution market.
- **Business Model:** B2B SaaS subscription for distributors (₹2,999/month per warehouse) + pay-per-voice order API fee.
